**What it is (30-sec pitch):**
> Pakistan's biggest business book — 200,000+ businesses across 420 cities and 130 categories, in a free app that works offline. Built for the people who *use* directories: freelancers, agencies, and business owners.

**The 3 USPs (nobody else in Pakistan offers these):**
1. **Lead Workspace** — save businesses to lists, track New → Contacted → Replied → Won, export CSV, and auto-draft personalized pitch emails/WhatsApp messages per business. *No Pakistani directory has outreach tools.*
2. **Free business listings, forever** — business owners list in 2 minutes, get a verified badge in 48h, get one-tap call/WhatsApp buttons and their own map pin. No pay-to-list.
3. **A real app, not a website** — installable on Android/iPhone in 5 seconds, works **offline** (200k listings in your pocket), bottom-nav app experience.
4. **Free business listings, forever** — two-sided: owners list free (verified badge, call/WhatsApp buttons, map pin) while freelancers get outreach tools — each side feeds the other's traffic.

**Volume stats (use these numbers):**
- 200,000+ listings — more than businesslist.pk (155k), Pakistan's biggest paid directory
- 420 cities & towns — every district HQ in Pakistan, all provinces + AJK + Gilgit-Baltistan
- 130 categories · 90+ flagship verified companies (HBL, PSO, AKUH, LUMS, Zameen…)
