About Yellow Pages Pakistan — the business directory of Pakistan

Yellow Pages Pakistan is a free online business directory (yellow pages) listing 200,000+ businesses across 420 cities and towns in Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan. Find manufacturers, suppliers, exporters, wholesalers, distributors, restaurants, hospitals, clinics, schools, colleges, software houses, digital marketing agencies, law firms, tax consultants, real estate agents, builders, couriers, salons and 120+ other categories — with phone numbers, WhatsApp, websites and Google Maps locations for every listing.

Search the Karachi business directory, Lahore business directory, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Sialkot or Gujranwala listings — or any of 400+ cities — free, with no signup. Business owners can list a business in Pakistan for free with 48-hour verification. Freelancers and marketing agencies get a built-in Lead Workspace: save Pakistani companies to lead lists, export contacts to CSV, and auto-draft personalized outreach pitches.

Frequently asked questions
What is Yellow Pages Pakistan?
A free business directory of Pakistan with 200,000+ listings across 420 cities and 130 categories — searchable phone numbers, WhatsApp and map locations.
How many businesses are listed?
Over 200,000 across every Pakistani city and district headquarters — Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and 417 more towns.
How do I find a company contact number in Pakistan?
Search by name, category or city, then tap Call. Mobile (03xx) and landline numbers are listed, with WhatsApp where available.
How do I add my business for free?
Open “List your business” — submit name, category, city and phone. Verification takes about 48 hours; listings are free forever.
Is there an app for Android and iPhone?
Yes — install it from the browser in about 5 seconds (no app store) and it works fully offline, with all 200,000 listings cached.
Where do freelancers find Pakistani clients?
In the Lead Workspace: shortlist businesses into lead lists, track outreach status, export CSV and auto-draft pitch messages.
Which cities are covered?
All 420 cities and towns, from Karachi, Lahore and Faisalabad to Ziarat, Gwadar, Skardu and Mirpur — every province plus AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan.
